Who Am I?

I am Keifer Miller

Survivalist, Nature Master, Mentor, Leader, Speaker Of The Trees

I've spent 30 years of my life living off grid. I've lived in tiny homes, caves, handbuilt mud houses, RVs, mountainsides, the area between the couch and the wall, you name it, I've made it a home. I've seen how this journey has improved my life and I'm ready to take the next step to show you how it can improve yours. Eight national book tours, 23 best-sellers, and sold-out TedTalks I am on a mission to help you realize the life you can live at your fullest potential, and help you find your way there.

If you'd like to heed the call to experience life as it was always meant to be, head over to my contact page and reach out so that we can begin your training and instructional lessons. Expect rigorous work that WILL challenge who you are and what you know, but I promise what will come out on the other end will be a hardened, battle-ready off grid hero who is ready for anything!
