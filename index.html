Trying to live off the grid? Here is everything you need to know!

To start, there are some key basics you need to always keep in mind:

  • Land

    When picking the right space to live off grid always bear in mind the access to water, the climate, the amount of sun exposure for solar panals, soil quality, and access to civilization in the case of emergencies.

  • Food

    For food you'll be best off growing your own food, big or small. A traditional garden is ideal with raised beds and a greenhouse for year-round growing. Meats and eggs are also viable food sources if you prepare for housing livestock.

  • Shelter

    Make sure you have the shelter you want in mind far before you pick your land, and be ready for the lifestyle that comes with whatever shelter you choose. You may want a temporary tiny home, or maybe a larger property you construct, but be sure to keep in mind the way you want your life to go and plan accordingly.

  • Power

    Living off the grid you obviously won't have access to traditional sources of power, so you'll have to find an alternative solution. The most popular options for power currently are solar panels or wind turbines, which are both very efficient at powering common homes and device usage. Bear in mind there may be periods of night with no sun or wind generation, so you'll need reserves and backups.

  • Water

    A clean water source will be an absolute necessity for living off the grid. It would be a good idea to either choose land with a nautral water source or where you could conveniently build a well. A water collection system like rain barrels also offer another solution.

Learn the tips and tricks and stay safe!
